Keeping you posted

You said, we did

The issues and questions you've raised with us, and what we've done about them. Newest updates first.

How it works

1

You raise it

At a meeting, by email, or in our Facebook group.

2

We take it on

We log it on our action tracker and take it to whoever can sort it: East Lothian Council, Police Scotland, or someone else.

3

We report back

We post updates here as things move on, until it's done or we've gone as far as we can.

Raise something with us

Is something bothering you about Wallyford, or is there something you'd like to see? Tell us, and we'll add it to the tracker.

If it's a problem that someone else is responsible for, like a pothole, a broken street light or antisocial behaviour, please report it to them as well. It gets fixed faster that way, and it makes our job of pushing for action easier.

We never publish names or personal details, and we may shorten or reword what was said.
