About Wallyford

A village built on coal

Wallyford grew up around its pits, its brickworks and its farms. The pits closed long ago, but the mining story is still everywhere: in the Miners' Stone, in the carved stones on the Bing, and in the names on the coal hutches that remember the 37 men who never came home.

Today the village is growing faster than at any time since, with new homes, a new primary school and the Wallyford Learning Campus.
