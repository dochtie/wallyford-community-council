Tenants' and residents' associations

A tenants' and residents' association (TRA) is a group of neighbours who get together to improve their street or estate. TRAs talk to the council, housing associations and developers on behalf of the people who live there, about things like repairs, shared spaces and estate management.

Neighbourhood Watch

Neighbourhood Watch schemes bring neighbours together to look out for each other, keep homes and streets safe, and share crime prevention news. A scheme can be as small as a few houses or a cul-de-sac.

Wallyford has four schemes, all in the St Clements Wells and Ravensheugh developments. Open a scheme to see its map and join. If your street isn't covered, Neighbourhood Watch Scotland can help you start a scheme, and you can sign up for free local alerts.

Wider partnerships

We represent Wallyford on these groups.

Musselburgh Area Partnership

Brings together community councils, East Lothian Council and local organisations to agree priorities for the Musselburgh area, including Wallyford, and decide how the local budget is spent.

About the Area Partnership

Community and Police Partnership

Regular meetings where we raise local concerns directly with Police Scotland, local councillors and council officers.

Association of East Lothian Community Councils

Community councils from across East Lothian working together on issues that affect us all.
