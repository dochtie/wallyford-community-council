About this website

Privacy and accessibility

What happens to your information when you use this website or get in touch with us, and how we've tried to make the website easy for everyone to use.

Your privacy

When you visit this website

  • We don't use cookies, adverts or visitor tracking, and you never need to sign in.
  • The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub keeps a technical record of visits, including your device's internet (IP) address, to keep the service secure. We can't see that record. GitHub's privacy statement says how it's used.
  • The dates and the You said, we did list are read from a Google Sheet each time you open a page, so your browser also contacts Google, which sees your IP address. Google's privacy policy says how it's used.
  • Links to other websites, such as Facebook, East Lothian Council and YouTube, take you to sites with their own privacy policies.

When you email us or raise something

  • We use your name, contact details and what you tell us only to deal with what you've raised and to reply to you.
  • Our email is a Gmail account, so messages are stored by Google. Only Community Council office bearers can read it.
  • If we need to pass your concern to someone who can act on it, such as East Lothian Council, a ward councillor or Police Scotland, we'll leave out your name and contact details unless you've said we can share them.
  • We never publish names or personal details on You said, we did, and we may shorten or reword what was said.
  • We keep emails only for as long as we need them to deal with the issue and keep a record of what we did.

Our meetings

Community Council meetings are held in public and minutes are taken. If you speak at a meeting, what you raise may be recorded in the minutes. Tell the Chair if you'd rather not be named.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email {{ site.email }}. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Accessibility

We want everyone in Wallyford to be able to use this website. We've built it so that you can:

  • use it on a phone, tablet or computer, and zoom in without the text spilling off the screen
  • get around it with just a keyboard, and skip straight to the main content
  • use it with a screen reader: pages have proper headings, and photos have descriptions
  • read it without animation, if your device is set to reduce motion

What we know isn't right yet

  • The outline that shows where you are when using a keyboard is hard to see on the pale parts of the pages.
  • The dates and the You said, we did list need JavaScript. Without it, you'll see our general meeting details instead.
  • The films we link to don't all have subtitles, and some documents we link to on other websites are PDFs that may not work well with a screen reader.

Tell us about a problem

If something on this website is hard to use, or you need information from it in another way, such as large print or read out over the phone, email {{ site.email }} or speak to us at a meeting, and we'll do our best to help.
