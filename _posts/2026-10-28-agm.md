Our Annual General Meeting was held on Wednesday 28 October in the Assembly Hall at Wallyford Learning Campus. Thank you to everyone who came along, and to our councillors, volunteers and supporters for all their work over the last 12 months.

Ward councillor Colin McGinn chaired the first part of the meeting.

## Your office bearers

- **Alli Coyle** was elected Chair. Ann Patton stepped down as Chair and remains a community councillor.
- **Sharon Jamieson** continues as Secretary.
- **Matthew Williams** joined the community council as a newly co-opted member and has taken on the role of Treasurer.

## Thank you, Beryl

Beryl Stevenson has retired after 40 years on the community council, most of them as Treasurer. She has been an amazing fundraiser and community champion. Over the years she sold horses at race nights in the Miners Club to raise money for the village Christmas lights, gathered donations, sold raffle tickets on gala day, represented Wallyford on the Musselburgh Area Partnership, and pushed for a community centre for the village.

> "A lot of people don't understand what a community council does – please come to our meetings and find out."
>
> Beryl Stevenson, speaking to the East Lothian Courier

[Read the East Lothian Courier article about Beryl](https://www.eastlothiancourier.com/news/26668851.wallyford-woman-retires-40-years-community-council/).
