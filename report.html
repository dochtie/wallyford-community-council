Five steps to a report that gets fixed

  1. Pin down the exact spot. "Outside 12 Masons Way, by the lamp post" beats "Masons Way". Use the map on the form if there is one.
  2. Take a photo if it's safe to. It shows the size of the problem and helps the crew find it.
  3. Say why it matters. Mention if it's near the school, a bus stop or a crossing, or if someone could get hurt.
  4. Keep the reference number you're given, and the date you reported it.
  5. Nothing happened? Contact your ward councillors with the reference number and ask them to chase it up.

Power, gas and water

Free, 24 hours a day, whoever your supplier is

Power cut 105 Puts you through to SP Energy Networks, who run the power lines in our area. Stay well clear of fallen or damaged power lines and call 999 if anyone is in danger. Check for power cuts
Smell gas? Carbon monoxide? 0800 111 999 National Gas Emergency Service, run by SGN in Scotland. Use this number whoever supplies your gas (Scottish Gas, Octopus or anyone else). Open doors and windows and get everyone into fresh air.
Water and sewers 0800 0778 778 Scottish Water, for burst pipes, leaks, no water, low pressure and sewer flooding. Contact Scottish Water

Not fixed? Contact your ward councillors

Tranent, Wallyford and Macmerry ward, East Lothian Council

Wallyford is in East Lothian Council's Tranent, Wallyford and Macmerry ward, which elects four councillors. They can chase up reports that have stalled and raise local problems inside the council. Include your report's reference number when you get in touch.

Tell us too

If a problem keeps coming back, or you've reported it and nothing has happened, let us know. We can raise it with East Lothian Council, or with Police Scotland at the Community and Police Partnership, and we'll post updates on our You said, we did page.

Please report it through the proper channel first. That's how problems get recorded, and the numbers help us make the case for action.
